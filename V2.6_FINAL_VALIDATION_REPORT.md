## 1. Project Objectives & System Overview

Autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) operating in mixed industrial-hospital environments must navigate dynamic corridors shared with personnel, transport carts, hospital beds, and forklifts. Traditional 2D SLAM and LiDAR navigation stacks lack semantic understanding, frequently suffer from false obstacle freezing, and fail to exploit directional environmental signage.

This project delivers a multi-tiered autonomous navigation and perception architecture:
1. **Perception Layer:** Fine-tuned YOLOv8n dual-head neural detector operating at $\ge 25\text{ Hz}$, recognizing 9 facility classes and directional signage.
2. **Sensor-Fusion & TTC Layer:** Spatial calibration mapping monocular RGB camera bounding boxes to 2D LiDAR range clusters, with odometry-based ego-motion compensation for radial Time-to-Collision (TTC) safety yielding.
3. **Topological Global Routing:** Multi-criteria A* graph search balancing path distance, dynamic obstacle density, narrow passage penalties, and SQLite historical failure penalties.
4. **Local Motion Control:** Nav2 Regulated Pure Pursuit (RPP) controller tracking curvature-regulated trajectories while maintaining collision-free clearance envelopes.
5. **Memory & State Engine:** Relational SQLite navigation memory recording segment metrics, dead ends, oscillation events, and transient blockage resets.

## 2. V2.5 Baseline Audit & Critical Diagnostic Findings

An independent technical audit of the V2.5 frozen baseline revealed that while the perception pipeline was fully verified (mAP@50 = 0.942, 0 FP on empty corridors), the autonomous navigation layer suffered from a **0/10 mission completion rate** in automated benchmarks due to three severe bottlenecks:

### Baseline Failure Mechanisms:
1. **Ego-Motion False TTC Yield Loop:** In `lidar_camera_fusion_node.py`, obstacle positions were tracked in the robot's local `base_link` frame without compensating for the robot's own forward motion ($v_{robot}$). Consequently, cruising forward past a stationary wall or hospital bed appeared as negative radial velocity ($dr < 0$), generating false TTC emergency stops ($\text{TTC} < 1.8\text{ s}$) that paralyzed the robot (95 false yields across 10 missions).
2. **TTC / Stuck Timeout Feedback Trap:** In `decision_engine_node.py`, the stuck timeout was hardcoded to $5.0\text{ s}$, while TTC safety yields lasted $1.8\text{ s}$. Repeated TTC yields kept the robot stationary, triggering false `NO_PROGRESS` stuck recoveries that forced unnecessary $45^\circ$ pivot maneuvers and replanning cascades (327 replans across 10 missions).
3. **Doorway Inflation Halo Overlap:** With `inflation_radius = 0.38 m`, standard $0.85\text{ m}$ facility doorways had overlapping cost halos, leaving only $0.09\text{ m}$ inscribed passage space ($< 0.36\text{ m}$ robot base), causing 32 local planner aborts in the Hospital ward entrance.
4. **Facility Spawn Orientation Traps:** Start coordinates in `config/navigation_locations.yaml` spawned the robot facing away from doorways directly into walls or obstacles (Hospital facing bed, Loading facing perimeter wall, Room A facing back wall).

## 3. V2.6 Phase 1 — TTC & Memory Reliability Fixes

V2.6 Phase 1 implemented surgical architectural corrections:
- **Ego-Motion Compensation Formula:** Derived true obstacle radial velocity relative to world frame:
  $$\dot{r}_{\text{true}} = \dot{r}_{\text{apparent}} + v_{\text{robot}} \cos(\theta_{\text{bearing}})$$
  Stationary obstacles now correctly register $\dot{r}_{\text{true}} \approx 0.0\text{ m/s}$ and are classified as `STATIC`, producing $\text{TTC} = -1.0\text{ s}$ (Safe).
- **Dynamic Obstacle Guard:** Enforced `obs.is_dynamic == True` and $dr < -0.15\text{ m/s}$ before engaging TTC safety yielding.
- **TTC Stuck Timeout Suspension:** The `last_progress_time` timer is actively refreshed during intentional safety yields, preventing false `NO_PROGRESS` recovery triggers.
- **Transient SQLite Blockage Reset:** Added `reset_transient_blockages()` to unblock temporary corridor obstructions between missions while preserving traversal metrics.

**Phase 1 Unit & Integration Test Verification:** All 5/5 targeted verification tests (`scripts/test_v26_phase1_fixes.py`) **PASSED** cleanly.

## 4. V2.6 Phase 2 — Navigation Reliability & Controlled Tuning

Phase 2 resolved local motion controller and costmap inflation chokepoints through evidence-backed sweeps:
- **Inflation Sweep ($0.38\text{ m} \to 0.30\text{ m}$):** Eliminated doorway inflation overlap. At $0.30\text{ m}$, the robot achieved 100% doorway transit success, preserved $\ge 0.27\text{ m}$ clearance across all facility zones, and achieved 0 collisions.
- **RPP Velocity Horizon Decoupling:** Reduced `max_allowed_time_to_collision_up_to_fast_stop` from $2.0\text{ s}$ to **$1.2\text{ s}$** and `approach_velocity_scaling_dist` from $0.60\text{ m}$ to **$0.45\text{ m}$**. The robot sustained its configured $0.50\text{ m/s}$ cruising speed ($0.472\text{ m/s}$ average, **$94.4\%$ speed efficiency** on straight spines).
- **Seamless Asynchronous Handoff:** Added `seamless=True` to `_dispatch_next_waypoint()`, eliminating the abrupt goal cancellations that previously caused $12.25\text{ s}$ ($22.2\%$) stationary lost time per 7 waypoints.
- **Oscillation Filter Hardening:** Increased `min_reversals` from 4 to 6 and `yaw_rate_thresh` from $0.10$ to $0.25\text{ rad/s}$, eliminating false pivot recoveries during routine heading alignment.

## 14. Remaining Limitations & Recommendations

1. **Adversarial Close-Range Sign Perception:** Directional signs viewed at distances $<1.0\text{ m}$ or angles $>45^\circ$ suffer from reduced single-frame recall ($35\% - 55\%$). In future work, multi-view feature fusion or active pan-tilt camera tracking can enhance off-angle recognition.
2. **Narrow Corridor Dynamic Cart Passing:** In $1.2\text{ m}$ corridors, an oncoming dynamic cart forces a full stop-and-yield. If the cart remains blocked for $>2.0\text{ s}$, the robot initiates an alternative detour. A "creep-ahead" negotiation protocol is recommended for future V2.7 revisions.
3. **Spawn Orientation Robustness:** Predefined benchmark poses require proper alignment towards room doorways. The finalized `navigation_locations.yaml` establishes verified spawn poses for future evaluations.
