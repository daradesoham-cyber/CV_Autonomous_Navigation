## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Verdict

A comprehensive forensic audit and live validation recovery were conducted on the V2.6 autonomous navigation system at commit `25bab3f` (tagged `v2.6.0`). The previously published claims of a **10/10 (100%)** standardized mission completion rate, zero false TTC yields, and 0.25 m doorway clearances in `V2.6_FINAL_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` have been evaluated through static artifact lineage tracing and empirical execution.

### Definitive Verdict:
1. **The Claimed 10/10 Benchmark Was Synthetic:** The historical benchmark files (`final_10_mission_results.json` and `.csv`) were generated by `scripts/compile_final_benchmark_results.py`, which compiled a static array of manually entered numbers. The claimed trajectory graphics in `report_evidence/v26_final/objective01_navigation/` were plotted by `scripts/generate_final_trajectories.py` using hardcoded waypoint arrays with added NumPy pseudo-random Gaussian noise (`np.random.normal(0, 0.03, ...)`).
2. **Empirical Benchmark Outcome on Commit `25bab3f`:** In an automated, machine-logged 10-mission benchmark on the live ROS 2 / Gazebo simulation stack (`scripts/run_v26_honest_validation.py`), the actual result is:
   - **Missions Completed:** **`0 / 10 (0.0%)`**
   - **Missions Stuck in Place:** **`5 / 10 (50.0%)`**
   - **Missions Timed Out:** **`5 / 10 (50.0%)`**
   - **Physical Collisions:** **`0`**
3. **Primary Root Cause:** A syntax error in `ros2_ws/src/autonomous_robot_navigation/config/nav2_params.yaml` nested `local_costmap` 4 spaces under `controller_server: ros__parameters:`. Nav2 consequently launched `/local_costmap/local_costmap` with default hardcoded parameters:
   - **Inflation Radius:** **`0.55 m`** (vs intended **`0.30 m`**)
   - **Cost Scaling Factor:** **`10.0`** (vs intended **`4.5`**)
   - **Global Frame:** **`map`** (vs intended **`odom`**)
   - **Rolling Window:** **`False`** (vs intended **`True`**)
   - **Observation Sources:** **`""` (EMPTY)** (vs intended **`scan semantic_obstacles`**)
   The $0.55\text{ m}$ local inflation radius created $1.10\text{ m}$ wide lethal/inscribed obstacle bands across all $0.85\text{ m}$ doorways, permanently trapping the robot inside rooms (such as the Hospital Ward and Room A) or causing local controller aborts upon approaching corridor doors.
