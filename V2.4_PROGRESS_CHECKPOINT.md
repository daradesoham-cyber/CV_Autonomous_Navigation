### Step C — Camera-LiDAR TTC Verification and Integration — COMPLETE — VERIFIED ✅
- **Topic:** `/vision/ttc`
- **Message Type:** `std_msgs/msg/Float32`
- **Publisher:** `lidar_camera_fusion_node.py` (`self.pub_ttc`)
- **Subscribers:**
  - `decision_engine_node.py` (`self.sub_ttc`)
  - `dashboard_backend.py` (`self.create_subscription(Float32, '/vision/ttc', ...)`)
  - `web/index.html` (Nearest Fused 3D Obstacle inspector card `#inspectObjTtc`)
- **TTC Calculation:**
  - For each tracked obstacle in the forward corridor (`pos_x > 0.05m`, `|pos_y| < 0.85m`), radial closing rate `closing_speed = -dr` (where `dr = (distance - tr['distance'])/dt`).
  - When closing speed > 0.05 m/s: `obs_ttc = distance / closing_speed`.
  - Publishes minimum TTC among all closing path obstacles. If no obstacles are closing, publishes `-1.0` (safe/infinite).
  - Also embedded in obstacle direction metadata: `TTC:{obs_ttc:.1f}s` on `/vision/semantic_obstacles` and `/fused_objects`.
- **Decision Engine Safety Behavior:**
  - **Threshold:** 1.8 seconds (chosen based on robot velocity 0.5 m/s and Nav2's 2.0s fast-stop parameter; triggering early yield at 1.8s prevents abrupt costmap lockup).
  - **Stale Timeout:** 1.5 seconds. If no TTC update received within 1.5s, data is treated as stale and ignored.
  - **Invalid Handling:** Values `<= 0` or non-finite are treated as non-closing (safe).
  - **Action:** Triggers proactive pause and yield (`_stop_robot()` and `dynamic_obstacle_pause_until = now + 1.8s`), logging `TTC ALERT`.
- **Compilation & Build:** Verified with `py_compile` and `colcon build`.
