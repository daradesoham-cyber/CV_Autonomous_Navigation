## 1. Executive Summary

During forensic analysis of the previous V2.6 benchmark, a genuine runtime benchmark produced **0/10 successful missions** (5 `STUCK_IN_PLACE`, 5 `TIMEOUT`), and Mission 6 (`HOSPITAL -> STORAGE`) failed in 100% of runs. In addition to a configuration nesting error in `nav2_params.yaml` (repaired in commit `e842749`), forensic geometry audits revealed severe **physical layout obstructions** and **hallucinated topological edges** in the simulated facility:

1. **Solid Concrete Crossway Walls:** `wall_cross_w` and `wall_cross_e` were 10.4-meter solid continuous walls across $Y = 1.0$ in the SDF world, blocking east-west transit between Junction 4, Storage, and Warehouse.
2. **Missing Emergency Exit Opening:** `wall_rec_div_w1` at $Y = -7.0$ was a continuous 8.2-meter solid wall with no doorway opening for the south-west exit corridor ($X = -12.5$), making the Emergency Exit completely inaccessible.
3. **Hospital Ward Doorway Chokepoint:** `hospital_bed_1` was placed at $(6.5, -8.0)$, leaving only $0.56\text{ m}$ of clearance to the doorway at $Y = -7.0, X \in [5.25, 6.75]$. The robot ($0.44\text{ m}$ diameter, $0.30\text{ m}$ Nav2 local inflation) was repeatedly trapped inside the ward.
4. **Warehouse Loading Bay Bottleneck:** `wh_rack_1` ($2.4\text{ m}$ long) and `wh_workstation_1` blocked the south transit lane and the entrance into Bay 1, leaving only 20 cm of clearance to `wall_cross_e`.
5. **Hallucinated Topological Graph Edges:** 6 bidirectional edge pairs (12 directed edges) cut straight through solid physical walls and furniture:
   - `junction_8 <-> vault_entry`: Cut through `lab_bench_1` and `wall_vault_e`.
   - `exit_corridor <-> exit`: Cut through solid `wall_rec_div_w1`.
   - `exit_corridor <-> dead_end_2`: Cut through solid `wall_utility_e`.
   - `wh_aisle_south <-> loading`: Cut through solid `wh_rack_1`.
   - `storage_approach <-> lab_bypass_entry`: Clipped within $0.17\text{ m}$ of `wall_storage_div_e` door jamb.
   - `junction_5 <-> dead_end_3`: Cut diagonally across `wall_archive_s`.

Route feasibility validation confirmed **110/110 edges PASS (100%)** with **0 Restricted** and **0 Colliding Edges**.
