## 6. Dead-End & Recovery Maneuvers with Rear LiDAR Check (Phases 10 & 11)

### Dead-End Detection:
When navigating into an alcove (`dead_end_2`, utility alcove at `(-14.0, -5.0)`):
1. Robot detects cul-de-sac node type.
2. Safe zero-velocity stop issued.
3. Coordinates persisted in SQLite `dead_ends` table.
4. Edge penalized by $10^4$ to prevent recurring traversal.
5. Backtracking motion initiated.

### Rear LiDAR Clearance Safety Check:
In V2, backtracking reversed blindly. In V2.1:
- The rear LiDAR sector ($|\theta| \ge 135^\circ$) is continuously evaluated.
- If rear clearance $< 0.35$ m, reverse motion is halted immediately, and the robot performs a safe $45^\circ$ pivot turn away from obstacles.
- If rear clearance falls below $0.30$ m during reverse motion, motion aborts instantly.

### Recovery State Machine:
Recovers autonomously under:
- `LOW_CLEARANCE`: Triggered when forward clearance $< 0.25$m or absolute minimum clearance $< 0.16$m.
- `STUCK_NO_PROGRESS`: Triggered when linear displacement $< 0.10$m for $> 5.0$ seconds during active navigation.
- `NAV2_ABORTED` / `GOAL_REJECTED`: Triggered on action client rejection or path blockage.

All recovery events are recorded to the SQLite `recovery_events` table with timestamp, coordinates, recovery type, actions taken, and LiDAR clearance.
